# 📱 ShakeExpense — Complete User Guide

Welcome to **ShakeExpense**, the smartest and fastest way to track your daily expenses and collaborate with your family. This guide explains how to get the most out of ShakeExpense.

---

## ⚡ 1. Adding an Expense Using Shake Gesture

1. **Shake Anywhere**: Gently shake your phone from any screen or your Android Home screen.
2. **Instant Quick Entry**: The Quick Entry overlay pops up over your screen.
3. **Select Category**: Tap one of the category chips (*Food, Transport, Groceries, Bills, Shopping, Entertainment, Others*).
4. **Enter Amount**: Use the large numeric keypad to type the amount (e.g. `250`).
5. **Tap Save (✓)**: Your expense is instantly logged and safely saved.

---

## ➕ 2. Adding an Expense Manually

1. Open **ShakeExpense**.
2. On the **Tracker** tab, tap the **`+` floating button** at the bottom right.
3. Choose your category, type your amount, and tap **Save (✓)**.
4. Your new expense appears at the top of the spreadsheet immediately with updated totals.

---

## 📊 3. Viewing Totals & Category Breakdown

- **Spending Summary Cards**: At the top of the Tracker tab, see your spending totals for **Today**, **This Week**, and **This Month**.
- **Category Breakdown**: Tap **Category Breakdown** to see an interactive visual progress bar showing where your money goes by percentage and total amount.

---

## 📅 4. Monthly History & Summaries

1. Open the **History** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Select any month to view total debits, total credits, and net spending for that calendar period.
3. Browse individual transactions organized chronologically by date.

---

## ✏️ 5. Editing or Deleting an Expense

1. In the **Tracker** spreadsheet or **History** list, tap on any expense row.
2. **To Edit**: Update the category, amount, or custom notes and tap **Save**.
3. **To Delete**: Tap the red **Delete** button and confirm. The expense is removed immediately and totals update.

---

## 🏦 6. Bank Notification Detection & Confirmation

1. When enabled, ShakeExpense automatically recognizes bank transaction SMS and notifications (e.g., UPI, HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay).
2. A confirmation banner appears at the top of your Tracker screen showing the detected amount and bank reference.
3. Tap **Confirm** to assign a category and log it with a single tap, or tap **Dismiss** to discard.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 7. Family Group & Parental Sync

### Creating a Family Group (Parent)
1. Tap the **Family** tab.
2. Tap **Create Family Group** and give your group a name (e.g., *Sharma Family*).
3. The app generates your official **6-character Invite Code** (e.g. `SHK-4892`) and **QR Code**.

### Inviting Family Members
1. Tap **Invite Member** in the Family tab.
2. Tap **Copy Code** or **Share Invite** to send the code to your child or spouse via WhatsApp/SMS.

### Joining an Existing Family (Child or Spouse)
1. Have your family member install ShakeExpense on their phone and sign in with Google.
2. Tap the **Family** tab $\to$ Tap **Join Family via Code / QR**.
3. Enter the 6-character Invite Code or scan the QR Code $\to$ Tap **Join Family**.
4. Both devices are instantly connected in real-time!

---

## 🔄 8. Offline Mode & Automatic Sync

- **Works 100% Offline**: You can add, edit, or delete expenses with zero internet connection.
- **Auto-Sync**: As soon as your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data, all pending records automatically synchronize with your Google account and family cloud.

---

## 👤 9. Google Account & Profile

1. Tap the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Sign in with Google** to permanently bind your expenses to your personal Google account.
3. If you ever switch devices or reinstall the app, simply signing into the same Google account automatically restores all your personal expenses and family data.

---

## 🛡️ 10. Permissions & Setup

1. Open the **Profile** tab and find the **Permissions & Setup** section.
2. **Shake Anywhere (Display Over Other Apps)**:
   - **Status**: ✅ **Enabled** or ⚠️ **Required**.
   - Tap **Open Overlay Settings** to allow ShakeExpense to pop up the instant Quick Entry keypad from your Home Screen or other apps.
3. **Bank Notification Detection (Notification Access)**:
   - **Status**: ✅ **Enabled** or ⚠️ **Required**.
   - Tap **Enable Notification Access** to allow ShakeExpense to read supported bank SMS/payment notifications on your phone for 1-tap confirmation.
4. **100% On-Device Privacy Guarantee**:
   - Notification parsing happens completely on your device. Raw notification contents are **never** sent to Firebase or any external server. Only confirmed transactions are saved as expenses.

---

## 🌓 11. Light & Dark Themes

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll to the **Theme & Appearance** section.
3. Choose **System Default**, **Light Mode**, or **Dark Mode**. Your preference is saved instantly.

---

## 🗑️ 12. How to Reset Expenses

1. Open the **Profile** tab and scroll to **Reset Expenses**.
2. Tap **Reset Expenses...** to open the period selector:
   - **Today**: Deletes all transactions logged today (00:00 to 23:59).
   - **This Week**: Deletes all transactions logged during the current week (from Monday to today).
   - **Specific Date**: Opens a calendar Date Picker to select a specific date for deletion.
3. Review the **Confirmation Dialog** showing the exact number of expenses and total ₹ amount that will be deleted.
4. Tap **Reset Expenses** to confirm, or **Cancel** to abort.
5. **Safety Guarantee**: Resetting affects **only** your personal expense records for the chosen period. Your Google account, family group, family members, categories, and settings are never deleted or affected. Synced deletions are automatically propagated to Firebase cloud storage.

---

## 🔒 13. Privacy & Security

- **Strict User Isolation**: Your personal expenses belong permanently and exclusively to your Google account.
- **Encrypted Cloud Sync**: All family and backup data is encrypted in transit and securely stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.
- **No Data Selling**: Your financial details remain private and are never shared with third parties.

## 🛠️ 14. Troubleshooting & FAQ

- **Shake gesture not opening on my phone?**
  - Make sure **Shake Anywhere** permission is enabled in Profile $\to$ Permissions & Setup.
  - On devices with aggressive battery savers (OnePlus, Xiaomi, Samsung), ensure background activity and notifications are permitted for ShakeExpense.
- **Are my expenses safe if I log out?**
  - Yes! Logging out simply ends your active session. All your historical expenses remain safely stored in the cloud and reappear as soon as you sign in again.
- **Do deleted expenses come back when I click sync?**
  - No! Deleted expenses are permanently recorded and removed from cloud storage so syncing will never resurrect deleted transactions.

## 🛡️ 16. Safe-to-Spend & Financial Safety Score

- **Configuring Safe-to-Spend**:
  1. Open the **Tracker** tab and tap the **Safe-to-Spend Setup** button.
  2. Enter your **Monthly Income** and optional **Savings Goal**.
  3. Safe-to-Spend automatically factors in your recurring bills and calculates your safe daily allowance (`Safe Today`).
  4. If no income is configured, the system displays ₹0 to prevent inaccurate calculations.
- **Financial Safety Score (0–100)**:
  - Located on your **Profile** tab.
  - Sub-scores: **Spending** (discretionary ratio), **Budget** (utilization), **Recurring** (fixed obligations burden), and **Risk Signals** (budget overruns).
  - All scores are derived 100% from your authentic transaction history.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 17. Shared Family Budgets & Child Exit Governance

- **Adding a Shared Family Budget (Family Pro)**:
  1. Open the **Family** tab.
  2. Scroll down to **Shared Family Budgets** and tap **+ Add Budget**.
  3. Choose a category (e.g., *Food*, *Groceries*, *Entertainment*) and enter the family-wide monthly limit.
  4. All family members' expenses in that category are aggregated against the limit with a real-time progress bar.
- **Child Member Exit Flow**:
  - Child members tap their profile in Family Hub and select **Request Exit**.
  - A persistent exit request is submitted, displaying an amber **Exit Requested** badge on the member card.
  - The Family Parent can open the member card and tap **Approve Exit & Detach** or **Dismiss Request**.

---

## 🔔 18. Spending Limit Alerts & Anti-Spam Engine

- When your personal or family spending crosses **70%**, **80%**, **90%**, or **100%** of your monthly limit, ShakeExpense delivers a heads-up alert.
- **Anti-Spam Intelligence**: The app only alerts you **once** per threshold each calendar month, preventing notification fatigue. Threshold states automatically reset on the 1st of each month.
